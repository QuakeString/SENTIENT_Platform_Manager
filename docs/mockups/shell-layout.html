Platform Manager: new layout

Round 2. Menu panel full height with the logo on top; fold button beside the name, and when folded the logo unfolds on hover. The menu and the coloured bar are see-through with a blur, switchable in preferences. Page titles stay in the page. The switcher dot shows the instance's health.

S1Remote instance, page at the topMenu shows the desktop behind it, blurred. Bar 72 px in the instance colour, see-through. Tiles as built today.
S2Scrolled, pointer on the fold buttonTiles pass under the bar, blurred. Unfolded, the fold button sits right of the name.
S3Menu folded, pointer on the logo64 px of icons and no button; on hover the logo turns into the unfold button. Item names show as tooltips. The choice is remembered.
S4This computer, Status page, a warningAmber dot: something needs a look (here disk space); hover says what. Thin progress strip under the bar for a running job.
S5Narrow windowNot enough room: the monitor bars drop, icon and value stay. Below 1000 px the menu folds by itself, as today.
S6See-through offSame page with the preference off: solid menu and solid bar.
S7About and preferences, new Appearance groupThe footer button (sliders icon) opens this page, as the (i) does today. Appearance: Theme and the see-through switch (on by default).
S9Theme picker in the barLeft of the bell. Its icon shows the choice: sun, moon, or half dark circle for Follow system.
D1Selected item joins the pageThe selected row takes the page colour and runs into the page, with curved corners above and below. Used in every unfolded state.
S8Switcher dotThe dot shows health, not the instance colour.
What changes against today
  • Logo and SENTIENT / PLATFORM MANAGER at the top of a full-height menu. Unfolded: a fold button right of the name. Folded: no button, the logo becomes the unfold button on hover.
  • Menu and bar are see-through with a blur (menu shows the desktop, bar shows the page). One switch in preferences turns both solid.
  • Bar 72 px instead of 52 px; it spans only the page area. Page titles and their refresh buttons stay in the page.
  • Monitors as icons with bar and value; when the window is narrow only icon and value.
  • Switcher dot: green all fine, amber needs a look, red a fault (SENTIENT stopped, a database down, backup failed), grey not reachable or still checking.
  • Menu footer: app icon, name and version, an Update pill when a new version exists, and a sliders button opening About and preferences.
  • About and preferences gets an Appearance group: Theme and See-through menu and bar.
  • Theme picker moves to the bar, left of the bell: sun for Light, moon for Dark, half dark circle for Follow system.
  • Add button reads "Add instance".
  • To confirm in the build: the blur. The desktop blur behind the menu depends on the system (Windows 11 and KDE support it); where it is not available the menu stays see-through without blur, or solid if that reads badly.
